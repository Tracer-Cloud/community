# Rules (Mirror)

> Mirror of the public rules listed in the Discord server.

1. Do not spam. Do not also make the moderators define spam, play nice. This includes recruitment posts and jokes/comments posted for the sake of trolling people.

2. No age-restricted or obscene content. This includes text, images, or links featuring nudity, sex, hard violence, or other graphically disturbing content.

3. Treat everyone with respect. Absolutely no harassment, witch hunting, sexism, racism, or hate speech will be tolerated, both towards people and towards other projects (including our own). Because of people's lack of ability to be civil, no politics are allowed here either.

4. If you see something against the rules or something that makes you feel unsafe, let staff know with #report. We want this server to be a welcoming space.

5. No discussion of crypto, betting, prediction markets, or trading financial assets. Ever. This rule also forbids jokes about these topics.

6. Use channels for their intended purposes. Need help with OpenSRE? Use #help. Need help on an issue you're contributing to? Use #contributors-helping-contributors. Do not post support questions in #general.

7. Read the troubleshooting docs before you ask questions: `<TBD: docs URL>`

8. No advertising or self-promotion ("Check out my project", "anyone need a developer", etc) outside of the #showcase channel without permission from a staff member. This includes DMing fellow members. Promotion is allowed in #showcase only. Additionally, no Discord invites should be shared at all, even within the #showcase channel.

9. Introductions in #introductions are for member introductions only. Promotional posts, links, or unrelated advertisements will be removed.

10. Our Community Staff can moderate how they see fit. If you have problems, go to #report and use the Report a Moderator form.
